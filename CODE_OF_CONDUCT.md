# Code of conduct

Sunroost is built by volunteers and users helping each other keep their solar systems running. Be respectful and
constructive in issues, pull requests, discussions and any other project space.

**Expected:** assume good faith, be patient with people new to solar or to software, give actionable feedback, and credit
others' work.

**Not acceptable:** harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, discriminatory language or imagery, publishing
someone's private information, and sustained disruption.

Maintainers may edit or remove content and restrict or ban participants who break these rules. Report problems to the
maintainers privately (see SECURITY.md for the contact channel); reports are handled confidentially.

This code is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
